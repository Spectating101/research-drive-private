# Data Vendorship Outreach Targets

Date: 2026-05-20

Purpose: turn the Sharpe data collection work into a concrete commercial outreach queue. This is not a spam list. It is a short list of public, official contact routes for platforms that explicitly connect data providers, research providers, or data product sellers with institutional buyers.

## Working Product

Do not pitch raw downloaded third-party market data as the product. Licensing is messy, and raw Yahoo/Refinitiv/Bloomberg-style redistribution is the wrong battle.

Pitch derived, documented, reproducible products:

1. Asia market intelligence panel
   - Asia listed equities, ETFs, index proxies, FX, commodities, crypto beta, and cross-asset return features.
   - Derived indicators, not raw vendor resale.
   - Best buyers: alternative data platforms, discretionary investors, consultants, academic researchers.

2. ASEAN/EM news shock taxonomy
   - Country-month and company-level news intensity, governance deterioration, policy shock, protest/corruption/macro stress signals.
   - Best buyers: asset managers, country-risk researchers, Smartkarma-style research platforms, academic data platforms.

3. Crypto/NFT landscape reference layer
   - CoinGecko/DeFi/OpenSea metadata, project history, chain/ecosystem mapping, category panels.
   - Best buyers: research/academic/demo use; weaker standalone institutional monetization than Asia/news signals.

## Minimum Pack Before Outreach

Prepare this before sending anything:

1. One-page product PDF: problem, coverage, sample fields, update frequency, provenance, limitations.
2. Data dictionary: columns, frequencies, entity identifiers, point-in-time status.
3. Tiny sample: 10-50 rows per product, enough to prove schema without giving away the dataset.
4. Demo chart or notebook: one insight, one backtest, or one country/company case study.
5. Compliance note: public-source provenance, no personal data, no MNPI, no raw market-data redistribution.

## What Counts As Real Validation

Do not count "interesting" replies as validation.

Count these:

1. They ask for a sample file or data dictionary.
2. They ask about history, update frequency, and identifiers.
3. They ask about trial terms, price, or exclusivity.
4. They introduce a data buyer, research lead, or vendor onboarding person.
5. They reject it but explain a missing feature buyers need.

If 3 of the first 10 targets ask for samples, continue productizing. If nobody asks for samples, the pitch or product is not concrete enough yet.

## Practical Pricing Test

Do not open with a high institutional price.

1. Free sample: 50 rows plus documentation.
2. Paid pilot: USD 500-2,000 for a one-off custom research/data pack.
3. Researcher subscription: USD 99-299/month if there is an academic/individual audience.
4. Institutional monitoring: USD 1,000-5,000/month only after there is a validated signal, clean delivery, and legal packaging.

## Legal/Commercial Guardrails

1. Do not sell raw Yahoo Finance, Refinitiv, Bloomberg, or other restricted market data.
2. Do not sell personal data scraped from job boards, marketplaces, or social sites.
3. Do not imply investment advice unless the product is framed and licensed correctly.
4. Keep provenance and source terms attached to every dataset.
5. Prefer derived indicators, metadata, entity mappings, and research outputs.
